# Prompts de Variações da Cidade (Layer 3)

Estes são 4 prompts criados para o Midjourney/DALL-E gerar variações do cenário principal (Layer 3) do jogo. Como solicitado, os letreiros estão exigindo o português.

### Variação 1: O Distrito do Hotel
> A classic 16-bit arcade beat 'em up background, pixel art style. An apocalyptic destroyed city street at sunset. The sky is dark and moody with a neon glow from distant ruins. In the background, a heavily damaged motel with a glowing neon sign that reads "HOTEL" in large bold letters. The perspective is side-scrolling flat, suitable for a 2.5D brawler game. High detail pixel art, dark cyberpunk atmosphere, empty scenery, no characters, just the environment background layer.
